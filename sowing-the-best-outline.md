# 8. Sowing the Best — Lesson Outline (Correct Slide Order)

## Introduction
1. **SOWING THE BEST** (title)
2. Now that we are healed and delivered from our old life, let us sow positive seeds to our family and friends.
3. *Galatians 6:7 (NIV)* — Do not be deceived: God cannot be mocked. A man reaps what he sows.
4. If we sow positive things, we will reap a good harvest.
5. Here are 5 things we can start to sow:

## 1. Sow Positive Attitudes
6. **1. SOW POSITIVE ATTITUDES**
7. **I. Be positive in your words** — *Ephesians 4:29 (NIV)* Do not let any unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building others up according to their needs, that it may benefit those who listen.
8. **II. Sow good works** — *Matthew 5:16 (NIV)* In the same way, let your light shine before men, that they may see your good deeds and praise your Father in heaven.
9. **III. Be kind and loving in how you relate to others** — *Ephesians 4:31–32 (NIV)* Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, brawling and slander, along with every form of malice. Be kind and compassionate to one another…

## 2. Sow Integrity
10. **2. SOW INTEGRITY**
11. *1 Timothy 4:7–8 (NIV)* Have nothing to do with godless myths and old wives' tales; rather, train yourself to be godly. For physical training is of some value, but godliness has value for all things, holding promise for both the present life and the life to come.
12. **Walking in integrity and godliness**
    - The Bible says, *"Train yourself to be godly."*
    - To be godly means to live a God-centered life.
    - Integrity is the quality of being upright morally.
    - To live in integrity means to live on what is right all the time. No compromise with sin.

## 3. Sow Spirituality
13. **3. SOW SPIRITUALITY**
14. *Matthew 5:14 (NLT)* You are the light of the world—like a city on a mountain, glowing in the night for all to see.
15. Sowing spirituality means you are bringing the presence of God into your home, school and workplace.
16. **How to sow spirituality?**
    - I. Manifest the Christ-like character
    - II. Bring prayer
    - III. Bring the Word of God
    - IV. Bring the cell group into your home, schools, and workplace

## 4. Sow Discipline
17. **4. SOW DISCIPLINE**
18. Discipline is self-control.
19. **Discipline means:**
    - Self-control; not impulsive and uncontrolled
    - Order and proper management
    - Sticking to right priorities and activities
20. *1 Peter 5:8 (NIV)* Be self-controlled and alert. Your enemy the devil prowls around like a roaring lion looking for someone to devour.
21. *1 Corinthians 9:27 (NLT)* I discipline my body like an athlete, training it to do what it should. Otherwise, I fear that after preaching to others I myself might be disqualified.

## 5. Sow a Correct Image
22. **5. SOW A CORRECT IMAGE**
23. To sow a correct image is to be at peace with yourself.
24. **Having a correct image means:**
    - You have a positive self-esteem.
    - You feel good about yourself.
    - You have a healthy outlook of who you are in Christ.
    - You see yourself the way God sees you: unique, precious, lovable and important.
    - You see yourself as a winner in Christ and not a failure.
25. *Psalm 139:13–14 (NIV)* For you created my inmost being; you knit me together in my mother's womb. I praise you because I am fearfully and wonderfully made; your works are wonderful, I know that full well.
26. *Philippians 4:13 (NKJV)* I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.

## Summary
27. **Sowing the Best:**
    1. Sow positive attitudes
    2. Sow integrity
    3. Sow spirituality
    4. Sow discipline
    5. Sow a correct image
